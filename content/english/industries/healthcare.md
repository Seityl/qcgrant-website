## Healthcare & Medical Services

**Protecting patient data and critical healthcare systems across the Caribbean.**

---

### Our Expertise in Healthcare

Healthcare organizations are prime targets for cyberattacks due to the sensitive nature of patient data and the critical importance of operational uptime. Q C Grant Ltd. provides specialized cybersecurity and IT services designed to protect hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, and medical practices throughout the Caribbean region.

Our healthcare expertise includes securing electronic health records (EHR) systems, implementing access controls for patient data, ensuring compliance with health information privacy regulations, and protecting medical devices and IoT equipment from cyber threats. We understand that downtime in healthcare isn't just inconvenient—it can be life-threatening.

With experience managing technology infrastructure for healthcare providers, we deliver solutions that balance stringent security requirements with the need for efficient clinical workflows and patient care delivery.

### Services for Healthcare Organizations

- **Healthcare Security Assessments** - Evaluation of patient data protection and system security
- **HIPAA & Privacy Compliance** - Guidance on health information privacy requirements
