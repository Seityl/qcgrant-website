## Our Story

Q C Grant Ltd was founded and is led by Charles D. Grant, a Caribbean-born IT professional whose career began in the early 2000s as a hardware technician and evolved into cybersecurity leadership and digital systems expertise. Over the years, Charles has cultivated deep experience in IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, and open-source technology, including overseeing the secure and reliable technology backbone for Dominica's largest pharmacy and applied that expertise through his own firm, QC Grant Ltd. The company is headquartered in Roseau, Dominica, and operates within the Information Communication Technology (ICT) sector, providing consulting and technology services tailored to local business needs.

The mission of QC Grant Ltd is to enhance cybersecurity, promote open-source solutions, and drive technological progress throughout the Caribbean region. This is grounded in a belief that secure, modern digital infrastructure and intelligent automation are essential for economic resilience and sustainable growth in Caribbean organizations from SMEs to larger institutions. Charles's professional ethos informed by industry certifications and advanced academic research in cybersecurity underpins the company's commitment to delivering enterprise-grade technology services with a focus on local context, operational reliability, and long-term capacity building.

In pursuit of this mission, QC Grant Ltd helps clients adopt robust security practices, implement open-source enterprise systems, and integrate workflow automation and AI into their operations, all aimed at reducing risk, improving efficiency, and strengthening digital transformation across the region. Its work reflects a broader vision to support Caribbean businesses and institutions as they modernize and secure their technology landscapes in an increasingly digital world.

## Our Commitment to Trust & Security

At QC Grant Ltd, trust and security are foundational to everything we do. We operate with a **security-by-design and ethics-first mindset**, ensuring that every solution we build or integrate prioritizes confidentiality, integrity, and availability from the outset. Our work follows recognized cybersecurity best practices, responsible disclosure principles, and strict access controls, with a clear separation between testing, production, and client data. We advocate for open-source technologies not for convenience, but for their transparency, auditability, and long-term security value.

We engage with clients as trusted partners, not just service providers. This means acting with integrity, respecting data sovereignty, and ensuring that clients fully understand the risks, controls, and trade-offs associated with their systems. QC Grant Ltd is committed to ethical practice, lawful conduct, and continuous improvement—because in a region where digital trust is critical to economic resilience, security is not optional; it is a responsibility.
